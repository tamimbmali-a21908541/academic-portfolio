# Digital Systems

> Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential logic — how computation is built out of gates.

**Grade:** 14/20 · **ECTS:** 6 · **Year:** 1 · **Institution:** Universidade Lusófona

---

## Topics studied

**Number systems**
- Binary, octal, hexadecimal, and conversion between them
- Signed representations and two's complement

**Combinational logic**
- Boolean algebra and logic gates
- Karnaugh maps and circuit minimisation
- Multiplexers, decoders, adders

**Sequential logic**
- Flip-flops and latches
- Registers and counters
- Finite state machines

## Why it matters for software

This is the bottom of the abstraction stack. Two's complement explains integer overflow; Boolean algebra explains bitwise operators; finite state machines are a design pattern that recurs everywhere from parsers to the reservation state machine in [Distributed Computing](../distributed-computing). The course feeds directly into [Computer Architecture](../computer-architecture) and the embedded work in [Advanced Computer Architectures](../advanced-computer-architectures).
