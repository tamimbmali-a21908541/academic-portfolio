# Discrete Mathematics

> Logic, sets, relations, combinatorics, and graph theory — the mathematics computer science is actually built on.

**Grade:** 14/20 · **ECTS:** 6 · **Year:** 1 · **Institution:** Universidade Lusófona

---

## Topics studied

**Logic and proof**
- Propositional and predicate logic
- Proof techniques — direct, contradiction, induction

**Structures**
- Sets, relations, and functions
- Equivalence relations and partial orders

**Counting**
- Combinatorics, permutations and combinations
- The pigeonhole principle

**Graphs**
- Graph representations, traversal, trees
- Paths, connectivity, and cycles

## Why it matters for software

This is the most directly applicable mathematics in the degree. **Induction** is how you prove a recursive algorithm correct. **Graph theory** is the model behind routing in [Computer Networks](../computer-networks), dependency resolution, and the relationship traversal in [Algorithms and Data Structures](../algorithms-and-data-structures). **Relations and set theory** are the formal basis of the relational model in [Databases](../databases) — a table *is* a relation, and SQL *is* relational algebra.
