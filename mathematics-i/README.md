# Mathematics I

> Single-variable calculus — limits, continuity, differentiation, and integration.

**Grade:** 16/20 · **ECTS:** 6 · **Year:** 1 · **Institution:** Universidade Lusófona

---

## Topics studied

- Limits and continuity
- Differentiation and its applications — rates of change, optimisation
- Integration techniques and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- Sequences and series

## Why it matters for software

Calculus underpins the numerical and analytical work later in the degree — the convolution and transform mathematics in [Signals and Systems](../signals-and-systems), the gradient-based optimisation behind machine learning in [Data Science](../data-science), and complexity analysis in [Algorithms and Data Structures](../algorithms-and-data-structures).
