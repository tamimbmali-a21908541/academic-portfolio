# Behavioral Competencies

> Professional and interpersonal skills for engineering practice — communication, teamwork, and self-management.

**Grade:** 16/20 · **ECTS:** 4 · **Year:** 1 · **Institution:** Universidade Lusófona

---

## Topics covered

- Communication — written, oral, and technical presentation
- Teamwork and collaborative working
- Time management and personal organisation
- Conflict resolution and negotiation
- Critical thinking and problem framing
- Professional ethics

## Why it matters for software

Most engineering failure is communication failure. The practices here were applied directly across the degree's group work — the requirements elicitation in [Requirements and Test Engineering](../requirements-and-test-engineering) is fundamentally a structured interviewing exercise, and the Agile user stories in [Software Engineering](../software-engineering) are a communication format before they are a planning one.
