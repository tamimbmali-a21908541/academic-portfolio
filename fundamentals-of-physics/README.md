# Fundamentals of Physics

> Classical mechanics, electricity, and magnetism — the physical basis of the hardware everything else runs on.

**Grade:** 16/20 · **ECTS:** 6 · **Year:** 1 · **Institution:** Universidade Lusófona

---

## Topics studied

**Mechanics**
- Kinematics and dynamics
- Newton's laws
- Work, energy, and momentum

**Electricity and magnetism**
- Electrostatics and electric fields
- Current, voltage, resistance, and circuit analysis
- Magnetic fields and electromagnetic induction

## Why it matters for software

Circuit theory is the prerequisite for building anything physical. The sensor and actuator work in [Advanced Computer Architectures](../advanced-computer-architectures) — voltage dividers, pull-up resistors, reading an analogue sensor — is applied electricity, and the wiring problems there were physics problems, not programming ones. Wave mechanics also underpins the signal theory in [Signals and Systems](../signals-and-systems).
