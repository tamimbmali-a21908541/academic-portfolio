# Mathematics II

> Multivariable calculus and differential equations — extending single-variable analysis to functions of several variables.

**Grade:** 17/20 · **ECTS:** 5 · **Year:** 1 · **Institution:** Universidade Lusófona

*The highest grade of Year 1.*

---

## Topics studied

- Functions of several variables
- Partial derivatives and the gradient
- Multiple integration
- Differential equations

## Why it matters for software

Partial derivatives and gradients are exactly the machinery behind gradient descent — the optimisation method underlying the neural networks studied in [Artificial Intelligence](../artificial-intelligence) and the regression models in [Data Science](../data-science).
